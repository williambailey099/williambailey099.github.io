OUR SOUND

Born from the garages of Sun City, California , Bologna Suntan was formed with one goal: to bring back the gritty, natural, unfiltered energy of 70s rock with a modern twist. We are influenced by Black Sabbath, Metallica, and the local underground scene that raised us.

Since summer of 2010, we have refined our noise into a movement that welcomes anyone who believes that rock and roll isn't just a genre, it's a lifestyle.
